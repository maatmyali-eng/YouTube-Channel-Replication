# Glossary

> **Purpose:** Plain definitions of the terms used across this repo, so every file means the same thing.
> **Use when:** A term in a prompt, checklist, or YouTube notice is unclear.
> **Time needed:** 1 to 3 minutes per lookup.
> **Needs:** Nothing.
> **Cost:** $0

## Inputs

- None for lookup. For P-OPS-07 (below): [TERM], [NICHE], [AUDIENCE], [LANGUAGE].

## Steps

1. Find the term in the alphabetical table.
2. If the entry carries a date, it is time-sensitive. Check the matching row in the [research snapshot](research-snapshot.md) before you rely on it.
3. If the term is missing, run P-OPS-07 and add the result to your own copy of this file.

Time-sensitive entries are marked "(verified 2026-10-21)" when re-checked against a current source on that date, or "(verified 2026-10-20)" when taken only from the repo snapshot.

## Prompts

### P-OPS-07 — Define a term for my channel

**Purpose:** Get a plain definition tied to your niche, with the policy risk named.

**Inputs:** [TERM] (the word or notice phrase), [NICHE], [AUDIENCE], [LANGUAGE].

```
Explain the YouTube term "[TERM]" to a beginner who runs a channel about [NICHE] for [AUDIENCE]. Write in [LANGUAGE].

Return:
1. A one-sentence definition in plain words.
2. One example from my niche.
3. What I should do about it before publishing, in two or three numbered steps.
4. Any policy or legal risk connected to the term. If the answer depends on current YouTube rules, thresholds, or dates, write "VERIFY BEFORE RELYING" and name the official YouTube Help page to open. Do not state numbers or dates from memory.
5. The closest related terms (three at most).

Keep it under 150 words. Do not promise income or approval.
```

**Expected output:** A short entry you can add to your own glossary copy.

**If the output is weak:** Add where you saw the term, and paste the exact sentence from Studio or the email.
