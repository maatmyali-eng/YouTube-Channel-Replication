# Compliance Master Checklist (C-01 to C-30)

> **Purpose:** One numbered checklist that keeps every video clear of the YouTube and copyright problems that get channels refused, claimed, or demonetized.  
> **Use when:** Before publishing any long video, Short, or thumbnail, and whenever you add a new tool, music source, or format.  
> **Time needed:** 15 minutes per video once you know it; 40 minutes the first time.  
> **Needs:** The finished video file or script, your thumbnail, your license log (a simple spreadsheet), and a browser.  
> **Cost:** $0

## Inputs

- [CHANNEL_NAME], [NICHE], [FACE_OR_FACELESS], [VOICE_MODE], [VIDEO_TOPIC] from your setup card ([01-setup-card.md](01-setup-card.md)).
- A **license log**: a Google Sheet or text file with columns Asset, Source URL, License name, Date checked, Screenshot saved (yes/no). Create it now. Items C-06, C-09, C-10, C-29 depend on it.
- Policy wording changes. Items marked "(verified 2026-10-20)" come from the research snapshot dated that day. Anything marked VERIFY BEFORE RELYING must be checked on the named YouTube Help page before you rely on it. I could not re-read live policy text while writing this file, so open the named page for every item that matters to your video.

## Steps

1. Create the license log (see Inputs).
2. Read all 30 items once. Notice which apply to your format. Mark the ones that do not apply as N/A in your notes with a one-line reason.
3. Before each publish, work through the items in order. Write Pass, Fail, or N/A beside each.
4. Fix every Fail. Re-check the item. Do not publish with an open Fail.
5. Use the one-page pre-publish gate at the end as the short version for each video. Folders 02 and 03 link to it.
6. Optional: run P-START-03 below to have an AI assistant walk you through the checklist against your actual draft. The AI helps you check; you make the decision.

## The checklist

How to read each table: **Rule** is what must be true. **Check (free)** is how to test it at $0. **Pass / Fail** is the exact wording you record.

### Group A: Reused, repetitive, and inauthentic content

Context (verified 2026-10-20): YouTube's July 2026 clarification names three non-monetizable categories: generic, repetitive, or template-based content with little variation between videos; off-putting or distressing content; and AI personas discussing sensitive topics. It targets mass production, not AI use as such. The reused-content policy is separate and unchanged. Faceless slideshow, stock-footage-plus-text-to-speech, and templated Shorts formats are high risk.

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | The video contains at least one element only you made: original footage, a test you ran, your own analysis, a teaching method, or a stated viewpoint. | Finish the sentence "Only I made ___" in your notes. Time stamp where it appears in the video. | Pass: named and on screen or in narration. Fail: blank or "the topic". |
| C-02 | The video is not a template with the topic swapped. It differs meaningfully from your last three videos. | Put the outlines of the last three videos side by side. Compare five features: hook type, section order, visual setup, example type, ending. | Pass: at least two of the five features differ from each of the other two videos. Fail: fewer than two differ. |
| C-03 | The video is not only stock footage or generated visuals with text-to-speech. | Add up seconds of original material (your footage, screen recordings, your drawn animation, your commentary). Divide by total seconds. | Pass: original material is at least 50 percent of runtime, or the commentary or teaching is clearly your own analysis. Fail: below 50 percent with no original analysis. (The 50 percent figure is this repo's working rule, not a YouTube number.) |
| C-04 | No third-party clips are reused, re-uploaded, or reacted to without added commentary, and no Short is cut from someone else's video. | Scrub the timeline. Every clip must be filmed by you, generated by you, or listed in the license log. | Pass: every clip traced to a source. Fail: any clip with an unknown or third-party source. |

### Group B: Copyright and Content ID

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-05 | Your script is original wording and original sequence, not a close paraphrase of anyone's script. | Run P-START-02 from [02-principles-and-copy-line.md](02-principles-and-copy-line.md). Also search three distinctive sentences from your script in quotation marks in a search engine. | Pass: verdict Clear and no exact matches. Fail: Too Close, or an exact match you did not quote on purpose. |
| C-06 | Every image, graphic, font, and clip you did not create is recorded with its license. | Open the license log. Match it to the timeline and thumbnail. | Pass: every non-original asset has a row with a source URL and license. Fail: any missing row. |
| C-07 | You use no quotations or clips of others' work unless you have decided to rely on fair use or an equivalent for criticism or commentary. Default is zero. | Search the script for quotation marks. Check each one. | Pass: zero, or each is under 15 words, attributed, and followed by your own analysis, with the risk accepted knowingly. Fail: any unattributed or unanalyzed quote. VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on YouTube Help "Fair use on YouTube". |
| C-08 | The uploaded video passes YouTube's copyright check before it goes public. | Upload as Private. In YouTube Studio, open the video's details page and read the checks result shown before publishing. Menu names change; VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on YouTube Help "Understand YouTube's copyright checks". | Pass: no issues found. Fail: any claim or issue. Replace the flagged asset. Do not dispute a claim unless you hold the license in writing. |

### Group C: Music and sound licensing

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-09 | Every music track comes from a source whose written license allows use in monetized YouTube videos. | Open the license page for the track. Save a screenshot. Log it. Prefer the YouTube Audio Library or a source that states commercial use is allowed. | Pass: written commercial-use permission logged. Fail: no license text, or "non-commercial", "personal use", or "for editing only". |
| C-10 | Every sound effect meets the same standard. | Same method. Check the license per file on sites that mix licenses (for example Freesound). | Pass: logged and commercial-use allowed. Fail: any "NC" (non-commercial) license, or no license. |
| C-11 | Attribution and extra conditions are met, and tracks that may be registered with Content ID have a saved license receipt. | Copy the required credit text into the description. Keep the screenshot. | Pass: required credits are in the description, and receipts are saved. Fail: attribution required but missing, or no receipt. |

### Group D: AI disclosure

Context (verified 2026-10-20): realistic altered or synthetic content must be disclosed in YouTube Studio under "Altered or synthetic content". YouTube can add the label itself since May 2026. Unrealistic, animated, or minor edits do not need disclosure. Sources disagree on whether AI voiceover and AI music need a label. VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on YouTube Help "Disclosing use of altered or synthetic content" and copy its current wording into your notes.

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-12 | Realistic AI-generated or AI-altered visuals are disclosed. | Use the decision table below for each AI asset. Open Studio, details page, and find the "Altered or synthetic content" question. | Pass: the toggle matches the table's result. Fail: the table says disclose and the toggle says No. |
| C-13 | AI voice is handled by the decision table. | Same. | Pass: matches the table. Fail: mismatch. |
| C-14 | AI music is handled by the decision table. | Same. | Pass: matches the table. Fail: mismatch. |
| C-15 | You record each disclosure decision and the reason. | Add a line to the per-video checklist: "AI used: visuals / voice / music / none. Decision: Yes or No. Reason: ___." | Pass: line completed. Fail: blank. |

**Decision table for visuals, voice, and music**

| Asset | Situation | Disclose in Studio? |
|---|---|---|
| Visuals | A realistic person appears to say or do something they did not say or do | Yes |
| Visuals | A realistic scene of an event or place that did not happen, or altered real footage so that it shows something that did not happen | Yes |
| Visuals | A realistic synthetic person or object presented as real footage | Yes |
| Visuals | Clearly animated, cartoon, stylized, or fantasy art that no viewer would take for real footage | No |
| Visuals | Minor edits: color grading, cropping, noise removal, captions, background blur, beauty retouching that does not change meaning | No |
| Visuals | AI-generated background or b-roll that looks like real footage and is presented as real | Yes |
| Voice | Voice that imitates or could be mistaken for a real, named person | Yes, and do not do it at all (see C-27) |
| Voice | Synthetic voice that reads your own script and is not presented as a real person's recording, with no real person's voice imitated | Sources disagree. VERIFY BEFORE RELYING. Default in this repo: choose Yes if a listener could take it for a real human's recording; otherwise record your reasoning in C-15 |
| Voice | Your own voice, with noise removal or EQ | No |
| Music | Instrumental music made with AI, not imitating a named artist | Sources disagree. VERIFY BEFORE RELYING. Default: no label needed for generic background music; log your reasoning |
| Music | AI music that imitates a real artist's voice or style so closely a viewer could think it is theirs | Yes, and do not use it (see C-27) |
| Music | Licensed library music, no AI | No |

### Group E: AI personas and sensitive topics

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-16 | No AI persona (avatar, synthetic host) delivers health, medical, financial, or similar advice as if it were an expert. Treat legal and mental-health advice the same way. | Read your format. Ask: "Is a synthetic character giving advice on health or money?" (verified 2026-10-20 for health and finance; legal and mental-health extension is this repo's caution) | Pass: no. Fail: yes. Change to a human presenter or a non-advice format. |
| C-17 | Sensitive-topic videos are informational, sourced, and reviewed by a human who understands the topic. | List sources in the description. Have a named human check every claim against a primary source. | Pass: sources listed and human reviewed. Fail: any claim without a source. A disclaimer alone does not fix a Fail. |
| C-18 | The video is not off-putting or distressing for shock value. | Watch the first 60 seconds with fresh eyes. Check for gore, graphic injury, disturbing generated imagery, or content that frightens children. | Pass: none. Fail: any. Remove or reframe. |

See [../05-originality-layer/03-sensitive-topics-and-personas.md](../05-originality-layer/03-sensitive-topics-and-personas.md) for detail.

### Group F: Misleading metadata and thumbnails

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-19 | The title's promise is fulfilled in the video. | Write the promise as one sentence. Find the timestamp where it is delivered. | Pass: delivered, and the first mention is within the first 60 seconds. Fail: not delivered or only partly. |
| C-20 | The thumbnail shows nothing that is not in the video. | Compare thumbnail elements to the video one by one. | Pass: every element appears in the video. Fail: any element does not. |
| C-21 | Description and tags are accurate and relevant. No keyword stuffing, no unrelated trending terms. | Read each tag and ask whether the video is about it. | Pass: all relevant. Fail: any unrelated term. |
| C-22 | No other channel's name, logo, or branding appears in your title, tags, thumbnail, channel name, or avatar to attract their viewers. | Search your metadata for the reference channel's name. Compare your avatar and banner against theirs side by side. | Pass: none, and your branding is clearly different. Fail: any use or close resemblance. |

### Group G: Child safety

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-23 | The audience setting in Studio is set honestly. Set "Made for kids" correctly. | In Studio, details page, find the audience question. Read YouTube Help "Set your channel or video's audience". VERIFY BEFORE RELYING. | Pass: setting matches whether the video is directed at children. Fail: mismatch or unanswered. |
| C-24 | No identifiable children appear without a parent's or guardian's written consent, and nothing shows minors in dangerous, sexualized, or exploitative situations or encourages dangerous challenges. | Scan the footage. Ask for each child who appears: "Do I have written consent?" | Pass: no children, or consent on file, and nothing unsafe. Fail: any gap. |

### Group H: Branded content disclosure

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-25 | Paid promotion, sponsorships, and free products received in exchange for a mention are disclosed using Studio's paid-promotion setting plus a spoken or on-screen disclosure. | In Studio, details page, find the paid promotion checkbox. YouTube can auto-label undisclosed branded content per announcements of 3 Sept 2026 (verified 2026-10-20). | Pass: disclosed, or no brand deal. Fail: brand relationship with no disclosure. |
| C-26 | Affiliate links are disclosed near the top of the description and aloud, and follow your country's advertising rules. | Read your country's ad-disclosure rules. In the US, search "FTC endorsement guides". VERIFY BEFORE RELYING for [COUNTRY]. | Pass: disclosed or no affiliate links. Fail: undisclosed links. |

### Group I: Likeness and impersonation

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-27 | No cloned voice and no synthetic face or body of a real person, living or dead, without that person's written permission. | Ask of every voice, face, and character: "Is this meant to sound or look like a real person?" | Pass: none. Fail: any. |
| C-28 | Real people who are identifiable in your footage have given permission where your country requires it, and your characters, names, and mascots do not copy another creator's. | Review footage for faces, name tags, and house numbers. Compare character designs to the reference channel's. | Pass: permission or no identifiable people, and designs are original. Fail: any gap. |

### Group J: Tool license terms

| ID | Rule | Check (free) | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-29 | Every tool that made part of the video allows commercial use of its output on the plan you used. | For each tool, open its terms. Search for "commercial". Log the license name, date, and a screenshot. See [04-free-tool-stack.md](04-free-tool-stack.md). | Pass: allowed and logged. Fail: not allowed, unclear, or unlogged. |
| C-30 | No tool watermark remains, and tool licenses are re-checked at least every 90 days and before using any new tool. | Look at all four corners of the exported video at full size. ## One-page pre-publish gate

Folders 02-long-form and 03-shorts link here. Copy this block into the video's notes and tick every box. One unticked box means do not publish.

```text
PRE-PUBLISH GATE — Video: [VIDEO_TOPIC]   Date: ________

ORIGINALITY
[ ] C-01 Original element named: ______________________ (timestamp ____)
[ ] C-02 Differs from last 3 videos on at least 2 of 5 features
[ ] C-03 Original material is at least 50 percent or analysis is mine
[ ] C-04 No third-party clips, no re-upload, no cut-down of others' video
[ ] C-05 P-START-02 verdict Clear; no exact-phrase matches

RIGHTS
[ ] C-06 License log covers every non-original asset
[ ] C-07 No unanalyzed quotes
[ ] C-09/10/11 Music and SFX licenses logged, credits in description
[ ] C-29 Tool terms allow commercial use; C-30 no watermark

DISCLOSURE
[ ] C-12/13/14 AI decision table applied; Studio toggle set to match
[ ] C-15 Decision and reason written down
[ ] C-16/17/18 No AI persona advice; sensitive claims sourced and human-checked; nothing distressing
[ ] C-25/26 Paid promotion and affiliate links disclosed

TRUTHFULNESS
[ ] C-19 Title promise delivered by ____ (timestamp)
[ ] C-20 Thumbnail matches video
[ ] C-21/22 Tags relevant; no other channel's name or branding

PEOPLE
[ ] C-23 Audience setting correct; C-24 children rules met
[ ] C-27/28 No cloned voice or face; permissions on file

FINAL
[ ] C-08 Uploaded private; copyright check shows no issues
[ ] A human watched the whole video start to finish
[ ] Publish or schedule manually in YouTube Studio
```

## Prompts

### P-START-03 — Compliance Walkthrough on a Real Draft

**Purpose:** Have an AI assistant apply items C-01 to C-30 to your draft, so you see gaps before the gate.

**Inputs:** [NICHE], [FACE_OR_FACELESS], [VOICE_MODE], [VIDEO_TOPIC], [VIDEO_LENGTH], plus [DRAFT_SCRIPT] (your full script or outline), [ASSET_LIST] (every music track, image, clip, and tool used, one per line, with source and license if known), and [THUMBNAIL_DESCRIPTION] (a one-paragraph description of your thumbnail and title).

```text
You are a YouTube compliance reviewer. You do not make legal or policy rulings; you find gaps for a human to check. Channel niche: [NICHE]. Format: [FACE_OR_FACELESS]; voice mode: [VOICE_MODE]. Video topic: [VIDEO_TOPIC], length [VIDEO_LENGTH].

My script or outline:
[DRAFT_SCRIPT]

My assets and tools, one per line:
[ASSET_LIST]

My title and thumbnail:
[THUMBNAIL_DESCRIPTION]

Review against these ten risk groups: (A) reused, repetitive, or template-based content; (B) copyright and Content ID; (C) music and sound licensing; (D) AI disclosure of visuals, voice, and music; (E) AI personas and sensitive topics such as health and finance; (F) misleading titles, thumbnails, and tags; (G) child safety; (H) sponsorship and affiliate disclosure; (I) likeness and impersonation; (J) tool license terms.

Output:
1. A table with columns Group, Finding (one sentence), Severity (Blocker / Fix / Note), Evidence (quote under 20 words from my inputs), and Fix I can do at $0.
2. A list titled "Cannot judge from what you gave me" naming each item you could not assess and exactly what information I should supply.
3. A table titled "AI disclosure" listing each AI-made asset in my inputs with your suggested Yes / No / Unsure for the Studio "Altered or synthetic content" question and the reason. For every Unsure, say VERIFY BEFORE RELYING and name the YouTube Help page "Disclosing use of altered or synthetic content".

Rules: Do not invent YouTube policy wording or numbers. If you are unsure of a policy detail, say VERIFY BEFORE RELYING and name the official YouTube Help page to check. Do not say any video is safe, guaranteed, or monetization-proof.
```

**Expected output:** A findings table sorted by severity, a list of unknowns, and a disclosure table.

**If the output is weak:** Add the missing source or license details to [ASSET_LIST] and rerun. If findings are generic, paste the script in sections and add "Review only group A and E for this section."

## Done when

- A license log exists and covers every non-original asset in your next video.
- All 30 items are marked Pass, Fail, or N/A for one real video, with every Fail fixed.
- The AI disclosure decision and reason are written in C-15.
- You have copied the one-page gate into your per-video notes.
