# Quick Start: First 24 Hours, First 7 Days, First 30 Days

> **Purpose:** One page that tells you what to do, in order, with a link to every file you need.  
> **Use when:** You have just cloned this repo and want to model one channel's style without reading everything first.  
> **Time needed:** 24 hours checklist about 3 hours; first 7 days about 12 to 18 hours; first 30 days depends on [WEEKLY_HOURS].  
> **Needs:** A Google account, a free YouTube channel, a text editor or Google Docs, and a free AI chat tool (see [free tool stack](../00-start-here/04-free-tool-stack.md)).  
> **Cost:** $0

## Inputs

- Your filled setup card: [CHANNEL_URL], [CHANNEL_NAME], [NICHE], [AUDIENCE], [LANGUAGE], [COUNTRY], [HARDWARE], [WEEKLY_HOURS], [FACE_OR_FACELESS], [VOICE_MODE]. If it is not filled yet, step 1 below does it.
- Prompt-specific placeholders, defined in P-OPS-01: [START_DATE], [PRIORITY_ORDER].

## Read this first (honest expectations)

Monetization has hard thresholds. As of the last check, the lower Tier 1 route needs 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and either 3,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months or 3 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days. The Tier 2 route (ads and Premium share) needs 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days (verified 2026-10-21; source: YouTube Blog, "What are qualified watch hours and Shorts views", 12 Aug 2026, and secondary guides at vidiq.com and air.io). For NEW applicants from 1 Feb 2027 the Tier 2 numbers rise to 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views; two secondary sources say the 1,000-subscriber requirement stays, so confirm on the YouTube Help partner program page before relying on it (VERIFY BEFORE RELYING).

Most brand-new channels will not cross these lines in 30 days. This repo cannot promise income. What the first 30 days can give you is a channel that is compliant, consistent, and measured, plus the non-YPP income routes in [parallel routes](../08-monetization/03-parallel-routes.md). Read [reality and timeline math](../08-monetization/01-reality-and-timeline-math.md) before you plan around a date.

## Steps

### First 24 hours (set up, no publishing yet)

1. **Fill the setup card.** Open [01-setup-card.md](../00-start-here/01-setup-card.md), copy it into a Google Doc named `yt-style-system-[CHANNEL_NAME]`, and fill every variable. Do not leave any blank; write "none" where it does not apply.
2. **Read the rules that protect the channel.** Read [principles and the copy line](../00-start-here/02-principles-and-copy-line.md), then [the compliance master checklist](../00-start-here/03-compliance-master-checklist.md). Print or pin the checklist.
3. **Pick your tools from the free list.** Read [free tool stack](../00-start-here/04-free-tool-stack.md). Install only what your [HARDWARE] can run. Write the chosen tool for each job (script, voice, video editor, thumbnail, captions) on the setup card.
4. **Secure the account.** Go to myaccount.google.com → Security → 2-Step Verification and turn it on. YPP applications require it. Then in YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility, complete phone verification. Studio labels change, so if a menu name differs, search Studio's help box (VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on the exact path).
5. **Make your tracking sheet.** Build the sheet from [tracking sheet](03-tracking-sheet.md). Enter today's subscribers, qualified watch hours, and Shorts views as the baseline row.
6. **Copy the two working templates.** Save [content calendar](../templates/content-calendar.md) and [per-video checklist](../templates/per-video-checklist.md) into the same Google Drive folder.
7. **Run P-OPS-01** (below) to turn your [WEEKLY_HOURS] into a dated plan.

Checklist:

- [ ] Setup card complete
- [ ] Compliance checklist read
- [ ] Tool for every job chosen
- [ ] 2-Step Verification on
- [ ] Tracking sheet baseline row entered
- [ ] Templates copied
- [ ] P-OPS-01 plan saved

## Prompts

### P-OPS-01 — Build my dated 30-day plan

**Purpose:** Turn the checklists above into a calendar that fits your real hours.

**Inputs:** [WEEKLY_HOURS], [FACE_OR_FACELESS], [VOICE_MODE], [HARDWARE], [NICHE], [VIDEO_LENGTH]; [START_DATE] (the date you begin, written YYYY-MM-DD); [PRIORITY_ORDER] (write "long-form first", "Shorts first", or "both").

```
You are a production planner for a small YouTube channel. Use only this information.

Channel niche: [NICHE]
Weekly hours available: [WEEKLY_HOURS]
Hardware: [HARDWARE]
On camera or faceless: [FACE_OR_FACELESS]
Voice mode: [VOICE_MODE]
Typical video length: [VIDEO_LENGTH]
Start date: [START_DATE]
Priority: [PRIORITY_ORDER]

Build a 30-day plan from the start date. Rules:
1. Each task is one line: date, task, estimated hours, the repo file that explains it, and the output I must save.
2. Never schedule more hours in a week than [WEEKLY_HOURS]. If the work does not fit, delete tasks in this order: automation, extra Shorts, extra thumbnail variants. Never delete a compliance, originality, or fact-check gate.
3. Include 3 public uploads within the 30 days.
4. Every publish day must be preceded by a gate day for the per-video checklist.
5. Put one weekly review on the same weekday each week.
6. Do not promise views, subscribers, or income. Do not suggest downloading, copying, or re-uploading anyone else's content.
7. End with a 5-line risk list naming the 5 tasks most likely to slip and what to cut if they do.

Output a Markdown table with columns: Date | Task | Hours | Repo file | Saved output.
```

**Expected output:** A table of about 25 to 35 rows that sums to no more than [WEEKLY_HOURS] per week.

**If the output is weak:** Paste your setup card again, state the exact number of hours you can spend on weekdays versus weekends, and ask for "a plan with fewer, larger tasks" if it came back too fragmented.

## Done when

- [ ] All three checklists above have every box ticked or a written reason it was skipped.
- [ ] You can name, from memory, the one rule that cannot be skipped: no other creator's actual words, footage, voice, music, thumbnails, logos, or likeness.
- [ ] Your tracking sheet shows a baseline and at least one weekly review.
