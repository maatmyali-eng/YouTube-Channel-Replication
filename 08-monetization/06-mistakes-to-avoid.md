# Mistakes to Avoid

> Purpose: List the tactics that cost channels their monetization, explain how each backfires, and give recovery steps for a strike or demonetization.
> Use when: Before you try any growth shortcut, at the Day 27 audit, and the day a policy notice arrives.
> Time needed: 30 minutes to read, 1 to 3 hours for a recovery.
> Needs: Your channel's Studio access and the notification email if you received one.
> Cost: $0

## Inputs

- [CHANNEL_NAME], [NICHE], [LANGUAGE], [COUNTRY]
- The notice or email from YouTube, if one arrived
- [../00-start-here/03-compliance-master-checklist.md](../00-start-here/03-compliance-master-checklist.md)

## Steps

### 1. Know the stakes

(verified 2026-10-21, support.google.com/youtube/answer/12950271) Most first-time violations get a warning. The next violation gives a Community Guidelines strike, and a strike expires after 90 days. A second strike within 90 days blocks posting for two weeks, including videos, live streams, stories, custom thumbnails and posts. Three strikes within 90 days terminate the channel. An active strike also blocks a YPP application, because the application needs a channel with no active Community Guidelines strike.

(verified 2026-10-20, snapshot; clarified 16 July 2026) YouTube's inauthentic-content policy names three non-monetizable categories: generic, repetitive or template-based content with little variation; off-putting or distressing content; and AI personas discussing sensitive topics such as health and finance. The separate reused-content policy did not change.

### 2. The seven tactics to avoid

| Tactic | How it backfires | Do this instead |
|---|---|---|
| View bots and paid views | Views from bots are invalid traffic. They do not count for real audiences, they distort your analytics so you cannot make decisions from them, and fake engagement violates YouTube's spam and deceptive-practices rules. AdSense also has invalid-traffic rules that can lead to account action. VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on AdSense Program policies, "Invalid traffic". | Spend the same hours on one better hook and one better thumbnail. |
| Sub4sub and subscriber exchanges | The subscribers never watch, so your impressions-to-view ratio and retention fall, and the YouTube system learns that your videos do not satisfy viewers. Subscribers do not add watch hours, and watch hours are the harder threshold. The practice violates the fake-engagement rules. | Ask viewers who finished a video to subscribe, and give a reason tied to the next video. |
| Reused content | Clips, compilations and re-uploads without original commentary, education or entertainment value fail the reused-content policy, and the channel is unlikely to qualify for YPP. | Make new scripts, new visuals and new audio in the style of the Style Bible. Use [../03-shorts/05-repurposing-without-reuse.md](../03-shorts/05-repurposing-without-reuse.md). |
| Deceptive metadata | Titles, thumbnails, descriptions or tags that promise something the video does not deliver violate the spam and deceptive-practices policy. Viewers leave early, retention drops, and a flag can bring a warning. | One clear promise per video, delivered in the first 30 seconds. |
| Mass templating | One template with only the topic word swapped is exactly what the July 2026 clarification calls repetitive or template-based content. A high upload count with low variation raises the risk, not the income. | Stay at your band's quota. Vary the angle, structure and hook. Score every video with [../05-originality-layer/02-scoring-rubric.md](../05-originality-layer/02-scoring-rubric.md). |
| Copied thumbnails | A copy of another creator's thumbnail, face, logo or character can bring a copyright or impersonation claim, confuses viewers about who made the video, and breaks the copy line in [../00-start-here/02-principles-and-copy-line.md](../00-start-here/02-principles-and-copy-line.md). | Copy composition rules (focal point, text size, contrast), then draw and write your own. See [../04-thumbnails/03-design-workflow-and-templates.md](../04-thumbnails/03-design-workflow-and-templates.md). |
| Fake giveaways | A giveaway that never delivers, or that asks for payment, personal data or logins, is a scam under YouTube's policies and under consumer law in most countries. Real contests are regulated in many places. VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on YouTube Help, "Contest policies", and your local contest law. | Skip giveaways during the first 90 days. If you run one later, publish written rules, deliver the prize and keep proof. |

Three more mistakes that cost channels money without breaking a rule:

- Hiding a sponsorship. YouTube may now label undisclosed branded content itself (verified 2026-10-21, announcement of 3 September 2026). Declare it first.
- Undisclosed realistic synthetic media. Disclose in YouTube Studio ("Altered or synthetic content") when the content is realistic and altered or synthetic. See [../00-start-here/03-compliance-master-checklist.md](../00-start-here/03-compliance-master-checklist.md).
- Promising results to viewers or clients. Do not state income figures, health outcomes or investment returns you cannot prove.

### 3. Recover from a warning or a strike

1. Do not delete the video yet. (verified 2026-10-21, support.google.com/youtube/answer/185111) If you delete a video, the strike stays and you cannot appeal it.
2. Read the notification email. It names the policy and the video.
3. Decide honestly whether YouTube made a mistake. If the video does break the rule, do not appeal. Accept the strike and avoid the cause for 90 days.
4. If you think it was an error, appeal from the link in the email or from YouTube Studio's content page. The official help page says you can appeal for 90 days after the warning or strike, and each strike only once. One secondary source gives longer windows, so use the shorter official one and VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on the page itself.
5. Check every other video on the channel for the same problem: same thumbnail style, same wording, same footage, same claim. Fix titles, descriptions and thumbnails now. Remove only the videos that cannot be fixed, after the appeal is settled.
6. For a warning, YouTube offers a policy training that lets the warning expire after 90 days. The warning does not expire if you violate the same policy inside that window (verified 2026-10-21).
7. During an upload block, do not post from another channel to get around the block. Evading a restriction can lead to more severe action. VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on YouTube Help, "Channel and account circumvention".
8. Copyright strikes are separate from Community Guidelines strikes and are counted separately (secondary source; VERIFY BEFORE RELYING). A copyright claim in Content ID is also different from a strike. Dispute a claim or send a counter-notification only if you hold the right to use the material. A false counter-notification has legal consequences. Seek local legal advice before you send one.
9. Log the strike date, the policy, what you changed and the expiry date (strike date + 90 days) in your tracking sheet. Re-run the Day 27 audit on the day the strike expires.

### 4. Recover from demonetization or a rejected YPP application

1. Read the reason in YouTube Studio under the Earn section and in the email. Common reasons are reused content, inauthentic or repetitive content, a policy violation, or thresholds not met.
2. Fix the cause before you ask for a review. A new application or appeal without changes repeats the failure.
3. Appeal or reapply only inside the permitted windows. (secondary source, revelator.com helpdesk, 2025, so VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on YouTube Help, "YouTube Partner Program overview & eligibility") The reported rules are: appeal within 21 days; reapply after 30 days if the first application was rejected; wait 90 days if a reapplication is rejected or you are suspended; and a two-month window to fix problems after demonetization.
4. For inauthentic or repetitive content, audit your last 20 uploads with the originality rubric. For each upload that fails, write what is missing: your own commentary, an original angle, a different structure or real expertise. Apply those fixes to the next 5 uploads before you reapply.
5. Be careful with deletion. Hours from deleted or private videos do not count toward YPP thresholds (verified 2026-10-21, YouTube source quoted by passionfru.it). Remove a video only if keeping it causes more harm than losing its hours.
6. If an appeal asks for an explanation of your process, describe it truthfully: what you script yourself, what tools you use, what you add that is original, and how you check facts. Never claim a process you do not use.
7. Stay off shortcuts while you wait. Every sub4sub or view-bot attempt during a review adds a second problem.

## Prompts

### P-MONEY-06 — Risk audit and honest appeal draft

Purpose: Check your channel against the seven tactics, and draft a truthful appeal or fix list after a notice.

Inputs: [CHANNEL_NAME], [NICHE], [LANGUAGE], [COUNTRY]; [NOTICE_TEXT] (the text of the YouTube email or Studio notice, or "none"); [VIDEO_LIST] (the last 20 video titles with one line on how each was made); [WHAT_I_DID] (a plain description of your workflow: what you write, what AI does, what tools you use, what you add that is original); [THUMBNAIL_PROCESS] (how you make thumbnails).

```
You are a careful YouTube compliance reviewer. You do not help hide problems, evade policies or write false statements. If a policy detail is not in what I paste, check it on an official YouTube Help page if you can search, and otherwise write VERIFY BEFORE RELYING and name the page. Do not invent policy wording, deadlines or numbers.

Channel: [CHANNEL_NAME]
Niche: [NICHE]
Language: [LANGUAGE]
Country: [COUNTRY]
Notice text: [NOTICE_TEXT]
Last 20 videos and how each was made: [VIDEO_LIST]
My workflow: [WHAT_I_DID]
My thumbnail process: [THUMBNAIL_PROCESS]

Task 1. For each of these seven risks, give Pass, Risk or Fail with one reason taken from my own text: view bots or paid views, sub4sub, reused content, deceptive metadata, mass templating or low variation, copied thumbnails, fake giveaways.
Task 2. For each of the three categories in YouTube's inauthentic-content clarification of 16 July 2026 (generic or template-based; off-putting or distressing; AI personas on sensitive topics such as health and finance), give Pass, Risk or Fail with a reason.
Task 3. If a notice is pasted, state which policy it names, the action taken (warning, strike, demonetization or rejection), and the next permitted step with its deadline. Mark any deadline you cannot confirm as VERIFY BEFORE RELYING.
Task 4. Write a numbered fix list ordered by risk, with the exact video or setting to change for each item.
Task 5. If an appeal is possible and my text shows the notice was a mistake, draft a short, factual appeal in [LANGUAGE] that describes only what I actually did. If my text shows the video did break the rule, do not draft an appeal. Write the fix plan instead and say why.

Output: one table for Tasks 1 and 2, the notice analysis, the fix list, and the appeal draft or the fix plan.
```

Expected output: Pass, Risk or Fail ratings with reasons, the notice analysis, an ordered fix list and either a truthful appeal or a fix plan.

If the output is weak: Add the exact titles, descriptions and thumbnail descriptions of the three weakest videos and ask for Tasks 1 and 4 again for those videos.

## Done when

- You can name all seven tactics and what to do instead of each.
- Your channel passes the Day 27 audit with no item marked Risk or Fail, or each remaining item has a dated fix.
- If a notice arrived, the strike or rejection date, the policy, the expiry or reapply date and the fix are all in your tracking sheet.
- No appeal or application states anything you did not do.
