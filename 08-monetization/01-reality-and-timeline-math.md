# Reality and Timeline Math

> Purpose: Work out honestly how long each YouTube Partner Program (YPP) tier takes, and what money can and cannot arrive in 10, 20 and 30 days.
> Use when: Before you promise yourself any monetization date, and again at every weekly review.
> Time needed: 45 minutes the first time, 10 minutes per re-run.
> Needs: A calculator or a spreadsheet, access to YouTube Studio if the channel exists, and the official pages named in Step 5.
> Cost: $0

## Inputs

- [COUNTRY] (where the channel is based and where the AdSense payment address will be)
- [NICHE]
- [WEEKLY_HOURS]
- [FACE_OR_FACELESS]
- Current channel numbers from YouTube Studio, if the channel already exists: subscribers, public watch hours in the last 365 days, Shorts views in the last 90 days, average view duration.

## Steps

### 1. Write down the thresholds you are working against

| Tier | What it unlocks | Subscribers | Uploads | Watch-hours path | Shorts path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 (fan funding, Shopping features) | Fan funding such as Super Chat, Super Thanks, memberships, and Shopping features where available in your country | 500 | 3 public uploads in the last 90 days | 3,000 public watch hours in 12 months | 3 million public Shorts views in 90 days |
| Tier 2, current (ads + Premium share) | Ad revenue share and YouTube Premium revenue share | 1,000 | not listed as a separate condition | 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months | 10 million public Shorts views in 90 days |
| Tier 2, new applicants from 1 Feb 2027 | Same | 1,000 (see note) | not listed | 8,000 public watch hours in 12 months | 20 million public Shorts views in 90 days |

(verified 2026-10-21) The Tier 1 and Tier 2 figures above match the research snapshot of 2026-10-20 and the sources I re-checked on 2026-10-21 (air.io, routenote.com, shacknews.com, switcherstudio.com, support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240).

Note on subscribers after 1 Feb 2027: two secondary sources state the subscriber requirement stays at 1,000, and one source says the announcement figures were given without a subscriber line. VERIFY BEFORE RELYING: open YouTube Help, "YouTube Partner Program overview & eligibility", and read the subscriber line for ads and Premium revenue sharing.

Rules that apply to both paths (verified 2026-10-21):

- Shorts views and long-form watch hours never combine. You qualify on one path or the other. Shorts watch time does not count toward the watch-hours path.
- Hours from private videos, unlisted videos, deleted videos, ad campaigns, and unlisted or deleted livestreams do not count.
- Account conditions: no active Community Guidelines strike, 2-Step Verification on the linked Google Account, and one AdSense for YouTube account linked to the channel.
- After you apply, review takes roughly one month.
- Tier 1 is only offered in countries on the expanded-YPP list. If [COUNTRY] is not on that list, there is no Tier 1 for you and the standard Tier 2 route is the only route. The list is on YouTube Help, "Overview of the expanded YouTube Partner Program" (support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240). YouTube emails you when the expanded program reaches you and you pass the thresholds.
- From 1 Feb 2027, members who earn from Shorts need 10 million Shorts views per rolling 90 days to keep Shorts revenue (verified 2026-10-20, snapshot; also reported on podderapp.com on 2026-10-21). Existing members are not removed by the new entry bar for ads and Premium.

### 2. Understand the 1 Feb 2027 change for a channel starting now

Today is 21 Oct 2026. Counting days: 10 days to 31 Oct, 30 in November, 31 in December, 31 in January. That is 102 days from today to 31 Jan 2027, the last day an application uses the lower thresholds (verified 2026-10-20: applying before 1 Feb 2027 uses the lower thresholds).

What that means in practice:

1. A channel with no audience today cannot realistically reach 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views in 102 days. The arithmetic is in Step 3. Plan as if you will apply under the 8,000-hour or 20-million-view bar.
2. Hours earned now still count later. The watch-hours window looks back 365 days, so hours earned in late 2026 count toward an application made after 1 Feb 2027 (verified 2026-10-21, relevantaudience.com).
3. Do not weaken quality to chase the date. A rejected application costs you a wait before you can reapply (VERIFY BEFORE RELYING: the reapply wait is stated on the YouTube Help YPP overview page). A channel that gets flagged for inauthentic content loses far more than it gains from applying a few weeks earlier.
4. Tier 1 thresholds (500 subscribers, 3,000 hours or 3 million Shorts views) did not change. If [COUNTRY] is on the expanded list, Tier 1 is the first realistic YouTube-paid milestone.

### 3. Do the arithmetic yourself

Formulas:

- Views needed on the watch-hours path = hours needed ÷ (average view duration in minutes ÷ 60)
- Minimum average daily views to ever qualify = views needed ÷ 365 (the window is 365 days, so a lower average can never reach the total)
- Daily Shorts views needed = Shorts views needed ÷ 90
- Views needed for a subscriber target = subscribers needed ÷ subscriber rate, where subscriber rate = subscribers gained ÷ views, read from your own Studio data

Worked numbers at a 4-minute average view duration (each view is 4 ÷ 60 = 0.0667 hours, so 15 views make 1 hour):

| Threshold | Views needed | Minimum average views/day over 365 days |
|---|---|---|
| 3,000 hours | 45,000 | 123 |
| 4,000 hours | 60,000 | 164 |
| 8,000 hours | 120,000 | 329 |

Days to reach each threshold at a steady daily view count (4-minute average view duration):

| Average views/day | 3,000 h | 4,000 h | 8,000 h |
|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 225 days | 300 days | never (365 days × 200 = 73,000 views = 4,867 hours) |
| 500 | 90 days | 120 days | 240 days |
| 1,000 | 45 days | 60 days | 120 days |
| 2,000 | 23 days | 30 days | 60 days |

If your average view duration is 8 minutes, every number of days halves. If it is 2 minutes, every number doubles.

Tier 1 and Tier 2 from zero in 102 days: 4,000 hours ÷ 102 days = 39.2 hours per day, which is 588 views per day at 4 minutes, every day, starting on day 1. A new channel ramps up slowly, so the later days would need far more than 588.

Shorts path, daily average over 90 days:

- 3 million ÷ 90 = 33,333 views per day
- 10 million ÷ 90 = 111,111 views per day
- 20 million ÷ 90 = 222,222 views per day

Subscribers can be the binding limit. At a 1% subscriber rate, 1,000 subscribers needs 1,000 ÷ 0.01 = 100,000 views. At 0.5%, it needs 200,000 views. These rates are illustrations, not benchmarks. Read your own rate in YouTube Studio under Analytics > Audience > Subscribers and Analytics > Overview > Views.

### 4. What is realistic in 10, 20 and 30 days

| Horizon | Realistic for a channel starting from zero | Not realistic |
|---|---|---|
| Day 10 | Setup finished, Style Bible written, 1 to 3 videos published, first Studio data (impressions, click-through rate, average view duration) | Any YouTube income |
| Day 20 | 3 to 6 uploads, a first read on which topics and packaging get impressions, a non-YPP route set up (see 03-parallel-routes.md), payout paperwork prepared | YPP application approved |
| Day 30 | Output quota met, a KPI baseline, a decision on what to double down on, possibly a first affiliate or service enquiry | YPP approved and paid |

Why YPP money cannot arrive in 30 days for a new channel: you need the thresholds first, then roughly one month of review, then earnings that accrue, then a payment threshold, then a processing period (Step 5). Each step is sequential. The only exception is a channel that already has an audience, for example one that already has 3,000 or more watch hours before today.

What can produce first money inside 30 days is outside YPP: an affiliate sale, a service client, a small digital product. Those are covered in 03-parallel-routes.md, and none of them is guaranteed either.

### 5. Payments and AdSense by [COUNTRY]

How payment works (verified 2026-10-21, support.google.com/adsense/answer/1709858 and answer/6189811):

1. After YPP acceptance, you link an AdSense for YouTube account. Earnings, tax details, identity verification and payment settings live in AdSense, not in YouTube Studio.
2. When earnings reach the verification threshold, you confirm your identity. Google then mails a PIN to the payment address in your AdSense account. You must enter the PIN before any payment is issued. You have 4 months from PIN generation to do it. In some countries an online identity check replaces the mailed PIN (secondary source; VERIFY BEFORE RELYING).
3. When earnings reach the payment-method threshold, you choose how to be paid. Available methods depend on your payment address. Options include Electronic Funds Transfer, wire transfer and country-specific methods. If a method is not on your "Add payment method" page, it is not available in [COUNTRY].
4. The default payment threshold is $100 (local-currency equivalent varies by country). If your balance reaches the threshold by the end of the month and there are no holds, a 21-day processing period begins. A balance below the threshold rolls over to the next month.
5. Money you earn in YPP is calculated from finalized earnings, so the first payment arrives well after the first view.

Facts to check for [COUNTRY], each marked VERIFY BEFORE RELYING until you fill the card below:

- The verification threshold amount (a secondary source says US$10; check the AdSense "Address (PIN) verification" page).
- Whether the bank account must be in the country of your AdSense payment address (a secondary source says yes).
- Tax information: Google asks for tax details in AdSense. For creators outside the United States this affects withholding on revenue from United States viewers. The rate depends on your country's tax treaty. Read YouTube Help, "Submit tax info to Google AdSense", and ask a local tax professional. This repo does not give tax advice.
- Bank charges and currency conversion on incoming transfers. Ask your bank before the first payment.

Fill this card once per channel:

| Item | Answer for [COUNTRY] | Official page checked | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is [COUNTRY] on the YPP availability list? | | YouTube Help, YPP overview & eligibility | |
| Is [COUNTRY] on the expanded (Tier 1) list? | | support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240 | |
| Payment methods offered | | AdSense Help, payment methods | |
| Payment threshold in local currency | | AdSense Help, payment thresholds | |
| Verification method (PIN or online) | | AdSense Help, PIN verification | |
| Tax form and treaty position | | YouTube Help, submit tax info | |
| Bank account requirement | | AdSense Help, add bank details | |

Time to first payment, formula: views needed to reach the payment threshold = payment threshold ÷ RPM × 1,000, where RPM is revenue per 1,000 views, read from YouTube Studio > Analytics > Revenue after you are in YPP. At an RPM of $1, a $100 threshold needs 100,000 monetized views. At $3, it needs 33,333. RPM varies by niche, country of viewers and format, so use your own number.

### 6. What no plan can guarantee

- That a specific number of views, subscribers or hours will arrive by any date.
- That YouTube approves your application. Reviewers judge the channel, including whether the content is original and has commentary, education or entertainment value.
- That a policy stays unchanged. The 1 Feb 2027 change shows thresholds move.
- That AdSense pays on a schedule. Verification, tax details, holds and bank problems all delay payment.
- That any affiliate, sponsorship or product route produces money in 30 days.

This repo raises your odds by making the content original, compliant and consistent. It cannot turn those odds into certainty.
