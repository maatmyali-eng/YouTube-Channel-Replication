# 30-Day Plan

> **Purpose:**  A day-by-day plan for days 1 to 30 with daily tasks, output quotas scaled to your weekly hours, and four weekly review points.  
> **Use when:**  You are starting a channel or restarting one, and you have finished 01-reality-and-timeline-math.md.  
> **Time needed:**  4 to 25 hours per week depending on your band (Step 1).  
> **Needs:**  The setup card filled in, the free tools from 00-start-here/04-free-tool-stack.md, and a tracking sheet.  
> **Cost:**  $0

## Inputs

- [WEEKLY_HOURS], [NICHE], [AUDIENCE], [LANGUAGE], [COUNTRY], [HARDWARE], [FACE_OR_FACELESS], [VOICE_MODE], [VIDEO_LENGTH], [CHANNEL_NAME]
- The country card from 01-reality-and-timeline-math.md
- A tracking sheet copied from [../09-operations/03-tracking-sheet.md](../09-operations/03-tracking-sheet.md)

## Steps

### 1. Pick your quota band

The quota is a ceiling. It is not a target to hit by skipping the quality gate. YouTube's inauthentic-content policy (clarified 16 July 2026, verified 2026-10-20) targets generic, repetitive or template-based content with little variation between videos. A higher quota with weaker variation puts monetization at risk.

Time assumptions, which you replace with your own logged hours at the Day 14 review: one long-form video takes 4.5 hours with the repo workflow, one Short takes 1.25 hours, and admin (analytics, comments, tracking) takes the remainder.

| Band | [WEEKLY_HOURS] | Long-form per week | Shorts per week | Admin hours per week |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 to 5 | 1 | 0 | 0.5 |
| 2 | 6 to 9 | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 10 to 15 | 2 | 3 | 1.5 |
| 4 | 16 to 25 | 3 | 5 | 2 |

- Under 4 hours per week: publish one long-form video every 2 weeks and no Shorts.
- Over 25 hours per week: stay in Band 4 and spend the extra time on research, scripting and editing quality, not on volume.

Totals this plan produces by Day 30 (arithmetic: Week 1 has one long-form video, Weeks 2 to 4 follow the band):

| Band | Long-form videos | Shorts | Total uploads |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| 2 | 4 | 6 | 10 |
| 3 | 7 | 9 | 16 |
| 4 | 10 | 15 | 25 |

### 2. Apply the week 1 slip rule

Week 1 is a one-time front load of about 12.5 hours: setup (1.5 + 1 + 2 + 1.5 hours), the first long-form video (2.5 + 2.5 + 1.5 hours). If [WEEKLY_HOURS] is below 12.5, you cannot finish Days 1 to 7 in a week.

Slip rule: first publish day = round up (12.5 × 7 ÷ [WEEKLY_HOURS]). Examples: 5 hours gives round up (87.5 ÷ 5) = 18. 8 hours gives round up (87.5 ÷ 8) = 11. 12.5 hours or more gives 7.

Keep the task order. Move the first publish to the slip day, and shift only the first video's dates. From the next Monday-equivalent, apply the band quota to the dates left. The Day 30 retro stays on Day 30 with whatever you have published.

### 4. Run the weekly review (Days 7, 14, 21, 28)

Spend 30 minutes. Write one line for each:

1. Output against quota: published count versus the band quota.
2. Gate: how many published items passed the gate on the first run, and which gate checks failed.
3. Originality: the average rubric score of the week's uploads.
4. Time: actual hours per long-form and per Short versus the assumptions.
5. Numbers: impressions, click-through rate, average view duration, new subscribers, and the watch hours or Shorts views total. Compare them with the thresholds in the KPI file.
6. One change for next week. Change one thing only, so you can tell what caused any shift in the numbers.

### 5. Do not apply to YPP inside this plan

This plan does not end in an application. Apply only after your numbers pass the thresholds in the timeline file and your Day 27 audit has no open items. Applying with weak content costs you the review wait.

## Done when

- Your band and first publish day are written in your tracking sheet.
- Video 1 is public by your first publish day, with the pre-publish gate logged.
- Four weekly reviews are logged, each ending in one change for the next week.
- Every upload passed the correct gate before publishing.
- The Day 27 audit list is empty by Day 29.
- The Day 30 decision is written down.
