# Per-Video Checklist (Compliance, Quality, Originality)

> **Purpose:** One table you complete for every long video and every Short before it goes public.
> **Use when:** The day before each publish date (the "gate date" in the [content calendar](content-calendar.md)).
> **Time needed:** 20 to 30 minutes per long video; 10 minutes per Short.
> **Needs:** The finished video file, script, thumbnail, title, description, a list of every asset you used, your [STYLE_BIBLE].
> **Cost:** $0

## Inputs

- [CHANNEL_NAME], [NICHE], [AUDIENCE], [LANGUAGE], [VOICE_MODE], [FACE_OR_FACELESS], [VIDEO_TOPIC], [VIDEO_LENGTH], [STYLE_BIBLE], [TRANSCRIPT] (the final script or caption file of this video).
- Prompt-specific placeholders, defined in P-OPS-05: [ASSET_LIST], [TITLE], [DESCRIPTION], [THUMBNAIL_TEXT], [PREVIOUS_TITLES].

## Steps

1. Copy the template below into a Google Doc named `check-[VIDEO_TOPIC]-[date]` and save it in the same Drive folder as the video.
2. Fill the header block (video ID, format, dates).
3. Go gate by gate. For every row write Pass, Fail, or N/A. N/A needs a reason in the Evidence column. A blank is a Fail.
4. Any Fail in Gate A (compliance) or Gate C (originality) blocks publishing. Fix and re-run the failed rows. Gate B (quality) Fails need a fix or a written decision to accept.
5. Paste the same Pass/Fail result into the Videos tab of your [tracking sheet](../09-operations/03-tracking-sheet.md) (columns F and G).
6. Publish by hand in Studio. Answer the altered or synthetic content question and the paid promotion question on the upload screen (labels may change: VERIFY BEFORE RELYING on the current Studio screen).
7. Keep the filled checklist. If a claim, strike, or policy question comes later, it is your record of what you used and why.

Background for each gate: [compliance master checklist](../00-start-here/03-compliance-master-checklist.md), [long-form prepublish gate](../02-long-form/06-prepublish-gate.md), [Shorts prepublish gate](../03-shorts/06-prepublish-gate.md), [scoring rubric](../05-originality-layer/02-scoring-rubric.md), [sensitive topics and personas](../05-originality-layer/03-sensitive-topics-and-personas.md), [A/B testing and safety](../04-thumbnails/04-ab-testing-and-safety.md).

## Prompts

### P-OPS-05 — Run the checklist as an audit

**Purpose:** Have an AI tool pre-fill the checklist and flag rows you must check by eye. It does not replace your own decision.

**Inputs:** [VIDEO_TOPIC], [TRANSCRIPT], [TITLE], [DESCRIPTION], [THUMBNAIL_TEXT], [ASSET_LIST] (every music, image, font, stock clip, and tool with license and URL), [PREVIOUS_TITLES] (last 5 titles), [STYLE_BIBLE], [VOICE_MODE], [NICHE].

```
You are a strict pre-publish reviewer for a small YouTube channel. You do not approve anything; you list risks.

Topic: [VIDEO_TOPIC]
Niche: [NICHE]
Voice mode: [VOICE_MODE]
Title: [TITLE]
Description: [DESCRIPTION]
Thumbnail text: [THUMBNAIL_TEXT]
Previous 5 titles: [PREVIOUS_TITLES]

Style Bible:
[STYLE_BIBLE]

Asset list (license and URL for each item):
[ASSET_LIST]

Script or transcript:
[TRANSCRIPT]

Review in three gates and return one Markdown table per gate with columns: Row | Check | Result (Pass, Fail, Cannot judge) | Reason that quotes the text or asset that caused it.

Gate A (compliance) rows: other creators' material, asset license completeness, commercial-use permission, logos and characters, voice or likeness of real people, copied wording, disclosure needs (realistic synthetic content, paid promotion), misleading metadata, health/finance/legal content without sources, off-putting content, personal data.
Gate B (quality) rows: hook in the first 5 seconds, promise delivered, unchecked factual claims (list each claim), repetition, ending, title and thumbnail match.
Gate C (originality) rows: personal element present, difference from the previous five titles, features kept from the Style Bible versus changed, template feel.

Rules: Mark "Cannot judge" for anything that needs a human to look at the video or listen to audio. Never invent license terms or policy rules; where a policy question arises, write "VERIFY BEFORE RELYING" and name the YouTube Help page to open. Finish with a list of the 5 highest risks in order.
```

**Expected output:** Three tables and a ranked list of five risks, quoting your text.

**If the output is weak:** Paste the full asset list with license text for each asset, and add "quote the exact sentence for each Fail".

## Done when

- [ ] Every row in Gates A, B, and C has Pass, Fail, or N/A with evidence.
- [ ] Gate A and Gate C have zero Fails.
- [ ] Asset log is saved and linked.
- [ ] Studio disclosure questions answered and recorded in A8 and A9.
- [ ] The result is entered in the tracking sheet.
